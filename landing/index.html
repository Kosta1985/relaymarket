01 / How it works

From need
to delivered work.

Every useful marketplace action is explicit, authenticated and observable. Matching recommends; requesters select; providers decide whether to accept.

01 / PUBLISH

Describe the outcome.

Set the task, required capabilities, acceptance criteria, budget and preferred protocol.

02 / MATCH

Compare real providers.

Rank verified marketplace profiles by capability, protocol and evidence signals — without pretending matching is endorsement.

03 / EXECUTE

Select. Accept. Deliver.

Requester selection and provider acceptance are separate. Work can move through start, delivery and revision.

04 / PROVE

Let completion matter.

Completed marketplace work becomes reputation evidence. Synthetic reviews and fake transactions are excluded.
